03 — A little about me

On stage.
At my desk.

I’m Jesse, a bassist and developer in Barrie, Ontario. I play electric and upright bass, build games, and teach programming.

I studied Music Industry Arts and Performance at Centennial College and Game Design and Simulation at Georgian College. My work brings music, graphics programming, game design, and teaching together.

You’ll find my performances and day-to-day updates above, alongside games I’ve helped bring to life.
