♫ MUSICIAN⌘ PROGRAMMER✚ GAMER

I’m Jesse, a bassist and developer based in Barrie, Ontario. I’m happiest somewhere between a bass line and a line of code.

I studied Music Industry Arts and Performance at Centennial College and Game Design and Simulation at Georgian College. My work brings music, graphics programming, game design, and teaching together.

Sometimes that means building a game. Sometimes it means picking up the bass. There’s always something to make.
